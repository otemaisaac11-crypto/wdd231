Helpful tips

Visa and entry

Check requirements before departure and keep your passport valid for at least six months.

Travel safety

Use reputable drivers, confirm park bookings, and keep emergency contacts accessible while moving between regions.

Currency

UGX is widely used, though cards are accepted in major cities and tourist lodges.
