Project Plan

Explore Uganda

A structured website plan for an immersive Uganda travel guide.

1. Site Name

Site Name: Explore Uganda

Reason for Name: The name "Explore Uganda" is clear, memorable, and directly conveys the purpose of the website. It serves as an inviting call-to-action for travelers wanting to discover Uganda’s natural beauty, national parks, and cultural heritage.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Explore Uganda is to provide an interactive travel destination guide for tourists planning a visit to Uganda. The site will feature popular attractions (national parks, waterfalls, lakes, mountains, and cultural heritage sites), practical travel tips, answers to frequently asked questions, and an inquiry contact form for tailored travel planning.

3. Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "What are the top national parks to visit in Uganda for wildlife viewing and gorilla trekking, and how do I get there?"
  • Scenario 2: "What travel requirements, safety tips, and best times of year should I keep in mind before planning my trip?"

4. Color Scheme

Primary Color: Deep Forest Green #1B4D3E

Used for header backgrounds, main page titles, primary buttons, navigation bar, and footer backgrounds.

Secondary Color: Warm Ochre / Sun Gold #D97706

Used for accent highlights, call-to-action buttons, card borders, and active link indicators.

Background / Neutral Color: Off-White / Sand #F8F9FA with dark text #1F2937

Used for the main page background and body text to ensure readability and contrast.

5. Typography

Primary Font: Montserrat

Used for all headings (<h1>, <h2>, <h3>), navigation links, and button labels for a bold, modern appearance.

Secondary Font: Open Sans

Used for paragraph body text, list items, FAQs, and contact form inputs for high legibility across mobile and desktop devices.
