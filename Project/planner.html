Smart Route Planning

Build your Uganda itinerary

Use this travel planner to select the kind of trip you want, identify must-see destinations, and map your best route through Uganda.

Itinerary ideas

  • 7 days: Kampala, Jinja, Lake Bunyonyi, and Queen Elizabeth National Park
  • 10 days: Bwindi, Lake Bunyonyi, and Murchison Falls
  • 14 days: Kidepo, Fort Portal, and the Rwenzori region

Suggested priorities

  • Wildlife lovers: Bwindi, Queen Elizabeth, Kibale
  • Adventure travelers: Sipi Falls, Jinja, Rwenzori
  • Culture seekers: Kasubi Tombs, Fort Portal, local markets

Travel checklist

  • Book park permits early
  • Pack layers for cool mountain evenings
  • Carry cash for rural areas and markets

Wildlife route

Start in Kampala, continue to Queen Elizabeth, then travel to Bwindi for gorilla trekking. Finish with a lake stay and cultural stop in the southwest.

Adventure route

Visit Jinja for rafting and the River Nile, continue to Sipi Falls, then explore the Rwenzori range for unforgettable hiking experiences.

Heritage route

Combine Kampala heritage landmarks, Lake Victoria, Fort Portal, and local cultural tours to experience Uganda beyond the safari trail.
